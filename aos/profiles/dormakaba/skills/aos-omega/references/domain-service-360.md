8. **Portals**: customer dashboard, service team workspace, Country Manager dashboard, approval inbox, system administration, reports/analytics, notification center.
9. **Regional separation**: validate country-specific data ownership, roles, commercial configuration and access; preserve independent UAE and KSA boundaries where the approved architecture requires it.
